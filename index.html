A hands-on, one-on-one learning experience designed to help you build confidence and develop professional acrylic nail skills.

DAY 01 Basics & Nail Preparation +

Nail anatomy, hygiene, sanitation, tools, product knowledge, acrylic basics, nail preparation, tips, shapes, priming and e-file introduction.

DAY 02 Acrylic Application +

Bead control, apex building, sculpting, full set application, French application, filing, shaping, structure and retention.

Model practice, gel polish, chrome, cat eye, tortoise shell, swirls, French, ombre, aura, foil, blooming gel, airbrush, rhinestones and 3D nail art.

Infill techniques and healthy, safe removal methods.
